The CTR-5000H is Carol Brass's flagship professional Bb trumpet, set apart by its hand-hammered raw brass bell. Left unlacquered, the bell develops a rich, living patina with every hour of play — darkening and personalizing over time in a way no lacquered horn can match. The result is a warm, centered tone with exceptional projection and resonance that players describe as more "open" and responsive than standard finished bells. Equally at home in jazz, orchestral pit, or studio work.
